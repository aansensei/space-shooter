WHY THE CURRENT VERSION FAILS
The character is drawn procedurally with rectangles on a small pixel grid
(GRID_W x GRID_H inside drawKanade()). The current shapes were placed by
guesswork rather than following any real proportion system, which is why
the head/body/hair ratios read as "off" even though every individual part
technically exists. Fix this by actually planning proportions before
drawing, the same way a character sheet would.

PROPORTION SYSTEM TO FOLLOW
Anime characters are typically 6-7 "heads" tall. Pick the head height as
your base unit (call it H) and lay out the rest of the grid against it:
  - Head (crown to chin): 1H
  - Neck + shoulders to waist: about 1.3H
  - Waist to hip/dress-hem: about 1H (this is where the oversized hoodie
    flares out, per Kanade_3rd_Anniversary.webp)
  - Hip to knee: about 1.5H
  - Knee to ankle: about 1.5H
  - Total: roughly 6.3H, which is correct for this kind of character (not
    a chibi, not hyper-realistic 8-head proportions either)
Increase GRID_W/GRID_H as needed so 1H maps to enough pixel rows to still
show detail (the current grid is too small to do the face justice — do not
be afraid to grow the buffer significantly, e.g. roughly double the current
resolution, and adjust the blit() scale factor down to compensate so the
final on-screen size stays about the same).
