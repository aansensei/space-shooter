4. HAIR MOTION AND MORE FRAMES
The hair still moves stiffly. Make it move like a chain:
- the swing should travel down the hair, so the roots barely move and the
  tips lag furthest (grow the lag with distance from the head, not one lag
  per mass);
- front side locks (hairMassH, sideL/sideR, now scaled by 0.7) and the
  fringe tips should swing a little on their own beat;
- the hair ends should lag the body's bob vertically too, not only sway.
"More frames": spriteKey quantises t to 24 steps per 900 ms cycle and hairT
to 48 steps per 1800 ms, so the sprite redraws about 27 times a second. One
uncached front redraw costs about 18 ms in the node bench and almost none
of that depends on RES, so simply doubling the steps would double the
cost. Do this in order:
  1) measure in Chrome (Performance panel, cutscene running, also with
     mobile CPU throttling at 4x);
  2) if there is headroom, raise the steps (t 24 -> 36 or 48, hairT to
     match) and check that the cache still hits when she stands still;
  3) if there is not, cache what does not move separately (for example the
     gown, legs and boots in their own buffer, redrawn only when lean,
     trail, droop, walkStep or castExt change) and redraw only hair, face
     and arms each step, then raise the steps.
Report the before/after ms per frame.
