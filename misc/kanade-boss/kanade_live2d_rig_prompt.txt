LIVE2D CHARACTER PREPARATION PROMPT — "Kanade" (25-ji, Nightcord de. style)
Reference: full-body character illustration, transparent background, front-
facing standing pose, one hand pulling an oversized hoodie collar up over
the nose/mouth.

============================================================
PART 1 — CHARACTER DESCRIPTION (for the artist/AI to match exactly)
============================================================
- Very long, straight, pale silvery-blue-white hair reaching past the hips,
  blunt-cut bangs with a few longer face-framing strands, no visible part
  line, subtle cool-grey shading in the hair shadows.
- Sharp, slightly hooded blue eyes, thin pale eyebrows, a calm/shy/guarded
  expression, small nose, mouth fully hidden behind the raised collar.
- Wearing a heavily oversized dark charcoal-grey zip-up hoodie cut like a
  flared dress: a popped/standing collar tall enough to cover the nose and
  mouth, long sleeves that fully swallow the hands (one hand emerges just
  enough to pinch the collar shut), a center-front zipper, a thin brown
  cord/ribbon tied at the neck under the collar, and a pleated, A-line skirt
  hem printed with a faded grey tone-on-tone poinsettia/lily floral pattern
  that fades out toward the waist, finished with a black trim band at the
  very bottom edge and black cuffs.
- Pale pink/blush shorts peek out just below the hoodie hem.
- Bare pale legs, black flat ballet-style shoes with a small strap/buckle.
- Holding a pair of on-ear headphones (matte deep purple/plum ear cups and
  headband) by the band, dangling loosely in her lowered hand.
- Pose: standing, weight shifted onto one leg, the other leg relaxed with
  the foot turned slightly outward; one arm bent up to hold the collar
  closed near the face, the other arm hanging down holding the headphones.

============================================================
PART 2 — WHAT TO DELIVER
============================================================
A fully layered source file (PSD or Clip Studio CLIP, NOT a flattened image),
canvas at least 2400 x 3400 px, 300+ DPI, transparent background, with every
part below on its own named layer or layer group, drawn in clean, closed
vector-style linework so Live2D Cubism Editor can generate ArtMeshes from
each layer directly. Where one part is hidden behind another at rest (a
shoulder under hair, an arm under the sleeve, the far leg partly behind the
near one), still draw that part's FULL shape, extended slightly past the
overlapping edge — Cubism has to move these independently, and a part that
was only ever drawn as a visible sliver will show a torn/cut edge the moment
it's rotated or shifted by physics. Name every layer/group in plain English
so a Cubism Editor operator can map them to ArtMeshes without guessing.

============================================================
PART 3 — REQUIRED LAYER / PART BREAKDOWN
============================================================

FACE
- FaceBase (skin, neck, ears if visible)
- Eyebrow_L, Eyebrow_R (separate)
- Eye_L and Eye_R, each split into: EyeWhite, Iris, Pupil, Highlight_Small,
  Highlight_Large, UpperEyelid_Open, UpperEyelid_Half, UpperEyelid_Closed
  (3 alternate shapes for blinking), LowerEyelid, EyelashesUpper,
  EyelashesLower
- Nose (small hint shape)
- Mouth (even though hidden by the collar at rest, draw a full mouth set in
  case a future "collar down" variant is wanted): Mouth_Closed, Mouth_Smile,
  Mouth_OpenSmall, Mouth_OpenMedium, Mouth_OpenLarge
- Blush_L, Blush_R (separate, so they can be toggled/faded independently)

HAIR (draw every strand generously longer/wider than its visible silhouette
so swinging or physics motion never reveals a hard cut edge)
- HairBack (the mass of hair behind the body, drawn all the way down past
  the lowest point it can visibly reach)
- HairFront_Left, HairFront_Right (the two long strands framing the face,
  down to the waist)
- HairBangs (the front fringe over the forehead)
- HairStrand_01 through HairStrand_04 (a handful of extra loose individual
  strands, each its own small mesh, for independent secondary-motion jiggle)
- HairShadowOnFace, HairShadowOnBody (soft semi-transparent overlay shapes
  where hair crosses in front of skin/clothing, kept separate so they can be
  repositioned as the hair moves without redrawing the base underneath)

ARMS / HANDS
- UpperArm_L, UpperArm_R (sleeve fabric, shoulder to elbow)
- Forearm_L, Forearm_R (sleeve fabric, elbow to wrist/cuff)
- Hand_Near (the one hand visible, pinching the collar - draw fingers)
- Hand_Far (fully drawn even though hidden inside the far sleeve, for future
  wave/gesture motions)
- Cuff_L, Cuff_R (the black ribbed cuff band, separate from the sleeve body
  so it can slide slightly with arm bend)

COLLAR / NECKLINE (must move independently from the torso, since it's being
actively held up by the hand)
- CollarFlap (the tall popped-collar piece covering the nose/mouth)
- NeckRibbon (the thin brown tie, drawn with a little extra slack for a
  soft dangle physics)

TORSO / HOODIE BODY
- TorsoFront (chest/stomach panel, zipper line included as its own thin
  overlay so it can flex slightly with breathing)
- TorsoBack (hidden at rest, still fully drawn)
- ZipperPull (the small separate zipper-tab shape, for a tiny independent
  swing)

SKIRT / DRESS HEM (split for independent pleat swing; keep the floral print
on its own multiply-mode layer above the base pleat shading so the print
doesn't stretch oddly when a pleat panel deforms)
- SkirtPanel_FrontCenter, SkirtPanel_FrontLeft, SkirtPanel_FrontRight,
  SkirtPanel_Back (hidden, still fully drawn)
- SkirtFloralPrint (separate overlay layer, same panel boundaries as above)
- SkirtHemTrim (the black band at the very bottom edge, separate so its
  thickness stays consistent even if the panels above it deform)
- ShortsPeek (the pink shorts visible just under the hem)

LEGS / FEET
- ThighUpper_L, ThighUpper_R
- Shin_L, Shin_R (split at the knee for a subtle idle weight-shift sway)
- Shoe_L, Shoe_R (with the small strap/buckle as its own tiny sub-layer)

HELD PROP — HEADPHONES (its own independent physics chain, since it just
hangs from one hand by the band)
- Headphone_Band
- Headphone_EarCup_Near, Headphone_EarCup_Far
- Headphone_Highlight (the small glossy plastic highlight on each ear cup,
  separate so it can stay visually "attached" as the prop swings)

============================================================
PART 4 — STYLE NOTES
============================================================
Soft cel-shading with one shadow tone and one highlight tone per major
color area (no painterly gradients that would be hard to separate). Palette:
muted lavender-grey charcoal for the hoodie, pale silver-blue-white for the
hair, pastel blush pink for the shorts, deep clear blue with a bright round
highlight for the eyes, matte plum/deep purple for the headphones, soft
warm-white for the skin. Line color should shift subtly per part (dark
brown-black for outlines, not pure black) to match the reference's soft
overall look.

============================================================
PART 5 — DELIVERABLE FORMAT
============================================================
1) One layered PSD or CLIP file with the exact groups above, each layer
   fully painted (not just outlines) and positioned in true character space
   (not scattered on a spritesheet).
2) A flattened reference PNG of the whole illustration for comparison.
3) A short manifest text file listing every layer name in the order given
   above, so whoever imports this into Cubism Editor can move quickly.
