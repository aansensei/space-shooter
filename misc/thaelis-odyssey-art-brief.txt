ART BRIEF: Thaelis, the Odyssey war gear (Gemini prompts)

HOW TO USE
1. Open Gemini image generation. Attach assets/images/game/enemies/thaelis-cocoon.png as the
   style reference with every prompt below.
2. Save each result into ONE folder under the file name given (armor.png, axe.png, phantom.png,
   sonic.png). PNG, JPG and WEBP all work. Any subset is fine: the game draws every piece in code
   until its file exists, so the axe alone is a valid delivery.
3. Run:  python3 misc/scripts/ingest_thaelis_assets.py <that folder>
   The script removes the backdrop, stamps the PNG metadata every game image carries
   (Author and Source = @aansensei), saves each file where the game loads it from, and lists it
   in sw.js and js/offline.js so it is cached for offline play.
4. Reload the game. Open the debug console, spawn Thaelis and press "Cast wave".
   If a piece sits off, tune its numbers in _THAELIS_ART at the top of
   js/render/enemy-thaelis-warrior.js (pivot, len, rFrac, widthR, tip).

HOUSE STYLE (applies to all four)
Painterly semi-realistic dark-fantasy game art like the attached cocoon: rich but slightly
desaturated colour, crisp readable silhouette, soft inner glow, hand-painted texture.
Palette: bronze and gold, deep crimson, white-hot edges. Not flat vector, not cartoon,
not pixel art. No text, no watermark, no border, no character holding the object.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AXE   save as axe.png   ->  assets/images/game/enemies/thaelis-axe.png   (main piece)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A single ancient Greek double-bladed battle axe (labrys), game sprite, lying perfectly
horizontal: the butt end of the haft at the far LEFT, the double blade head at the far RIGHT.
Side view, flat orthographic, no perspective, no tilt. The axe fills about 90% of the image width.

Haft: dark weathered olive wood with bronze bands and a bronze butt cap. A leather-wrapped grip
sits about one sixth of the way in from the butt end.
Head: two large crescent blades mirrored exactly above and below the haft (symmetric top/bottom),
forged from dark crimson-red metal with a white-hot glowing cutting edge, a bronze centre boss
holding a ruby, faint Greek meander engraving on the blade faces, thin glowing red cracks of energy.

Style: painterly semi-realistic dark-fantasy game art, same rendering as the attached reference
(rich but slightly desaturated colours, crisp silhouette, soft inner glow, hand-painted texture).
Not flat vector, not cartoon, not pixel art.

Background: one flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF, perfectly uniform edge to edge. No gradient, no
shadow, no floor, no glow spilling onto the background, no hand, no character, no text, no border.
Wide 16:9 image.

In game: the axe hangs beside the shield, rises and trembles while Ulysses' Return gathers, then
chops. The code pivots it on the grip (17% of the width from the left) and lights the blade
centre (86% of the width). The ingest script crops to the axe, so only its own proportions matter.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARMOUR RING   save as armor.png   ->  assets/images/game/enemies/thaelis-armor.png
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A ring of ancient Greek armour plates seen straight from the front, game sprite, perfectly
centred, with 8-fold rotational symmetry. Eight large pointed bronze plates (like spaulders or
sun rays) point outward, one every 45 degrees, each with a crimson enamel stripe, a gold rim and
a small ruby at its root. Between them eight short crimson horns. The plates are riveted to a
thin crimson and bronze band.
THE CENTRE IS EMPTY: the inner 65% of the ring's diameter is a hole filled with the same flat
magenta as the background, because the game's golden shield shows through it.
The whole ring fits inside the image with a small margin. Square 1:1 image.
Background and hole: flat solid pure magenta #FF00FF, uniform, no gradient, no shadow, no glow
spill, no text.

In game: laid over the octagonal shield and turning with it. The shield's radius is 36% of the
image width (rFrac), so the hole must be at least that wide.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BA KHI PHANTOM   save as phantom.png   ->  assets/images/game/effects/thaelis-bakhi-phantom.png
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A towering ghost of an ancient Greek warrior king (Odysseus) made only of crimson and gold
battle aura, seen from the front, upper body only, chest at the exact centre of the image.
Crested Corinthian helm with two white-hot eye slits, broad armoured shoulders, the right arm
raised high holding a double-bladed axe above the head, the left arm holding a large round shield.
He is translucent and made of flame: deep red body, glowing orange-gold rim light, licks of fire
rising off the crest, shoulders, shield and blades. The body dissolves into smoke below the waist.
The figure spans about 40% of the image width at the shoulders and nearly touches the top.
Square 1:1 image.
Background: flat solid pure BLACK #000000 everywhere, no stars, no ground, no text.

In game: drawn additive (black vanishes) behind Thaelis and behind its Cocoon while a wave is
gathered, about three times the body's size, then it lifts away.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SONIC WAVE HEAD   save as sonic.png   ->  assets/images/game/effects/thaelis-sonic-arc.png
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A crimson sonic shockwave flying to the RIGHT, game VFX sprite. One thick bright crescent arc,
bulging to the right like a closing parenthesis ")", white-hot at its core and crimson at its
edges, tall enough to span almost the full image height, its leading edge about 70% of the way
across the image. Behind it, to the left, three or four thinner fading ripple arcs of the same
shape, like sound waves, with streaks of red energy and sparks trailing to the left.
Nothing to the right of the leading crescent. Image ratio 5:8 (taller than wide).
Background: flat solid pure BLACK #000000, no text.

In game: drawn additive as the head of the Ulysses' Return wave, 224px tall, its leading edge
(tip = 0.70 of the width) on the wave front.

NOT NEEDED
No full character, no helm or cape on the body, no UI. The cast cracks in the ground, the flame
auras (ba khi, dau khi), the Iron Body plates and the lane are drawn in code.
